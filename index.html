Privacy Policy & Data Covenants

The protocols governing our commitment to absolute spiritual and digital privacy.

Notice of Free and Private Association

The House of Strauss is a private, unincorporated international organization acting as a Decentralized MicroChurch. As a spiritual assembly of believers, we operate entirely apart from digital commerce, data harvesting, or any secular enterprise designed to monetize personal information. By navigating our resources, you acknowledge and agree that your interaction is covered by the fundamental human right to private free assembly, thought, and religious observation.

1. Data Minimization & Abstraction

We believe the physical Temple and the living soul require absolute autonomy regarding personal information. The House of Strauss employs a strict policy of Data Minimization. We do not deploy cookies, pixels, or trackers. We do not—and will permanently never—sell, rent, or distribute any information regarding our Members, Ambassadors, or public visitors to third parties.

2. Communications Protocol

Any data submitted through our "Secure Communication" portal is encrypted and routed exclusively to the internal servers of the House of Strauss administration. Emails and physical correspondence are routinely purged in accordance with our administrative covenants once the pastoral or structural matter has been resolved.

3. International Jurisdictional Acknowledgment

The House of Strauss is governed primarily by ecclesiastical and private trust covenants. Any secular dispute regarding the administration of this website or data covenants shall be subject exclusively to private, binding arbitration as designed by the Board of Trustees, rejecting public secular trial.
